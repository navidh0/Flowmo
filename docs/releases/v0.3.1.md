# Flowdo 0.3.1 — calendar details, and a mini clock on minimize

**Choose the first day of the week.** Settings → Calendar lets you pick any day from Sunday to Saturday (Monday is the default). The Week and Month views follow it, and so does "This week" on the Stats page.

**Weekday names on dates.** The Week heading, the Today and Upcoming groups ("Tomorrow · Sat, Sep 26"), a task's due date, your session history and the daily chart now say which day of the week a date falls on.

**The mini widget appears when you minimize.** Minimize Flowdo, or close it to the tray, and the small timer shows up in the bottom-right corner of your screen until you bring the window back. Drag it wherever you like and it remembers the spot. You can turn this off in Settings → System; if you pin the widget with "Show mini widget", it stays open all the time as before.

**Your data** stays in `%APPDATA%\Flowdo`. Updating does not touch it, and uninstalling does not delete it.

**Windows will warn about the installer.** Flowdo isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". The portable build runs without installing.
