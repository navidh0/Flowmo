# Flowdo 0.5.0 — light theme, chimes, and layout you can arrange

**Flowdo now comes in light and dark.** Settings → System → Theme offers System (following your OS), Light, or Dark. Both windows — the main window and the always-on-top mini widget — switch instantly with no flash, and all text meets WCAG AA contrast (at least 4.5:1) in both themes. Project and calendar colors look the same in both, with dark text on top so they're readable either way. System theme follows your OS settings live.

**Phase ends now chime instead of beeping.** When a focus phase finishes, Flowdo plays a short synthesized chime: a rising three-note chime when focus ends, a soft falling bell when a break ends. (A Flowmodoro focus you end with Stop also chimes, since that's how a Flowmodoro session finishes.) Stopping or skipping a phase early, or a phase interrupted by your computer sleeping, stays silent. The sounds are generated in the app—no audio files to ship—and play only when Settings → Behavior → Sound is on, independently of notifications. A "Test sound" button in Settings lets you hear both before enabling them.

**Reorder the panels.** Settings → Layout lists your three panels (Projects, Timer, Tasks) with Move left and Move right buttons so you can put them in the order that works for you. The arrangement is saved and restored on restart. "Reset layout" still restores the default order and widths if you want to start over.

**Compact density.** Settings → Layout → Density picks between Comfortable (the default) and Compact. Compact tightens spacing throughout the app by 20%, useful on small screens and with long task lists. It applies to both the main window and the mini widget.

**The mini widget is now resizable.** Drag its edges to change its size, from the old minimum (220×88) up to 480×200. The countdown grows with it, and once the widget is tall enough it also shows the title of your current task. Its new size is remembered, just like its position.

**Layout fixes.** The timer panel no longer scrolls sideways at the smallest window sizes, and the earned-break card and switch-mode confirmation now fit inside the timer panel instead of spilling past it.

**Also in 0.4.2, and so here too.** The mini widget has a close button (×) in its top-right corner, and Flowdo's icon shows in the Windows taskbar again instead of a blank placeholder (and on Linux window managers that showed none).

**Electron 44.** The app now runs on Electron 44, up from 43.

## Installing

Download a build from the project's GitHub Releases page. Windows and Linux installers (NSIS on Windows, AppImage on Linux) update automatically from 0.4.x. Portable and deb users download 0.5.0 from the releases page.

**Your data** stays in `%APPDATA%\Flowdo` on Windows and `~/.config/Flowdo` on Linux. Updating does not touch it, and uninstalling does not delete it.

**The installers are unsigned.** On Windows, SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The AppImage and `.deb` are unsigned too; most Linux setups won't warn about that, but it's worth knowing if a package manager or AppImage launcher does flag it.
