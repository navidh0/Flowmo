# Flowdo 0.5.2 — your sessions on top of your calendar

**Sessions now draw on top of your calendar events.** In the Day and Week views, focus and break sessions used to sit in narrow side-by-side columns next to any calendar event they overlapped, often squeezed down to thin slivers. Now calendar events form the background layer at full width, and your sessions draw on top of them. You see what you planned and what you actually did in the same place. Events that overlap each other still sit side by side.

**Events and sessions now look different.** Calendar events appear as a light tint of the calendar's colour with a solid stripe on the left edge. Sessions stay solid: the project colour, grey without a project, teal for breaks. This makes a session unmistakable from an event, even when a calendar and a project share a colour. Hovering an event still shows its full details, even where a session covers part of it.

**All-day events and the Month view are unchanged.** Stats are also unchanged: totals only ever counted your sessions, never calendar events.

**Your data** stays in `%APPDATA%\Flowdo` on Windows and `~/.config/Flowdo` on Linux. Updating does not touch it. If you're on 0.4.x or 0.5.x, Flowdo updates itself; open Settings → Updates to check now.

**The installers are unsigned.** On Windows, SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
